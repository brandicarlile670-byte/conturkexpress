Delete your account

Want your ConTurk Shipping app account closed? Email us and we will delete it — there is no form to fill in and no charge.

Send your request to

contact@conturk.com

Email deletion request

What to include

  1. 1Write from the email address registered to your account, so we can verify the request is yours.
  2. 2Use "Account deletion request" as the subject line.
  3. 3Give your full name, your company and the phone number registered to the account.

What happens next

  • We confirm we received your request within 3 business days.
  • Your account is closed and your personal data is deleted within 30 days at the latest.
  • We email you once the deletion is complete.

Shipping, customs and accounting documents that Turkish law requires us to keep are retained for their statutory periods after deletion. They are not used for any other purpose.
